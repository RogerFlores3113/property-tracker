Stricter rules when Claude reads websites for you

The stricter rules are {{ "on" if strict else "off" }}. When they are on, Claude and Codex may only use text you paste or a page you opened and asked them to read, and may not crawl listing sites on their own. They are also limited to {{ strict_per_call }} places per call and {{ strict_per_hour }} places per hour.

Some sites forbid automated reading in their terms, so check a site's terms before having an AI read it. By default Claude and Codex may read, search or scrape listing pages with their own tools and hand what they found to Property Helper (the app itself opens no listing pages). A change takes effect right away.
