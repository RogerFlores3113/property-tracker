What Claude can and can't do

  • It can add places, move them forward through the stages, log notes and calls, book tours, add contacts, and read your searches and what matters to you to help you compare. It never deletes anything and never changes your own notes.
  • It can't delete anything. Only you can delete, here in this window.
  • Your data stays on this computer. Claude only sees what it asks for, and sends that to its own AI model.
